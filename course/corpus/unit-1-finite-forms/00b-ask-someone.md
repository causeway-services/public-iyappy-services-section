## Your goal

Ask one person whether they have an object.

Use **object + waŋží + luhá + he**. The question article **waŋží** keeps possession hypothetical; **luhá** refers to the person you are asking. The final **he** marks this question.

For example, **Wíyatke waŋží luhá he?** asks about a cup. A positive answer changes **waŋží** to **waŋ**, and the speaker uses **bluhá**.

| Object | Lakota |
| --- | --- |
| shirt | ógle |
| hat | wapȟóštaŋ |
| knife | míla |
| bowl | wakšíškokpa |

In the tasks, sometimes you ask the question and sometimes you answer your partner. Pay attention to who is speaking.

[Question and response patterns: printed page 18; article explanation: printed page 20.](https://wotakuye.weebly.com/uploads/2/3/7/4/23749479/ldl121_packet.pdf#page=19)
