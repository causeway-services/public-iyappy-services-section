## Your goal

Ask and answer about a named person.

For someone else, use their name before the object and **yuhá** for **has**.

The packet's example **Sunshine háŋpa waŋží yuhá he?** asks whether Sunshine has a shoe. Its positive reply keeps the same name and uses **waŋ**: **Háŋ, Sunshine háŋpa waŋ yuhá.**

In these tasks, work with **Sunshine** and **Nacole**, the names used in the source examples. A reply about Sunshine stays about Sunshine; it does not change to **I**.

| Object | Lakota |
| --- | --- |
| spoon | čhiŋšká |
| fork | wíčhapȟe |
| plate | wakšíča |
| jacket | ógle šóka |

**Ógle šóka** is one vocabulary item written as two words. Keep both words together when building your sentence. You will also reuse objects from the earlier lessons.

[Third-person question and reply: printed page 19; vocabulary: printed pages 12 and 18.](https://wotakuye.weebly.com/uploads/2/3/7/4/23749479/ldl121_packet.pdf#page=20)
