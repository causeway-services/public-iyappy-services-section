## Your goal

Answer a question about something you have.

A partner asks **Wíyatke waŋží luhá he?** — “Do you have a cup?”

You can reply **Háŋ, wíyatke waŋ bluhá.** — “Yes, I have a cup.”

The object goes before **waŋ bluhá**. In a positive reply, **waŋ** marks an actual thing and **bluhá** refers to the speaker: **I have**. **Háŋ** means **yes**.

| Object | Lakota |
| --- | --- |
| cup | wíyatke |
| book | wówapi |
| belt | iphíyaka |
| rock | íŋyaŋ |

Use the same sentence pattern with a different object. Your partner's **luhá** asks about **you**; your reply uses **bluhá** because you speak about **yourself**.
